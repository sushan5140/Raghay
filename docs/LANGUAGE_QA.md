# Marathi Mate — Language & Curriculum QA

## Status

Reference-based linguistic consistency pass completed for the full intermediate milestone.

This audit is designed for a learner-facing product, not as a claim of formal CEFR certification or native-editor certification.

## Romanization convention

Marathi Mate uses a readable ASCII learner romanization rather than a strict academic transliteration system.

High-frequency spellings are kept stable across the course:

- Mi — मी
- Mala — मला
- Majha / Majhi — माझा / माझी
- aahe / aahet — आहे / आहेत
- nahi — नाही
- jaeen — जाईन
- yeen — येईन
- kareen — करीन
- bheteen — भेटीन
- shakto / shakte — शकतो / शकते
- mhanun — म्हणून
- karan — कारण
- peksha — पेक्षा
- jast — जास्त
- kami — कमी

Romanization is a scaffold only. From later units, Devanagari becomes primary and romanization is hidden or secondary.

- verb + ऊ + शकतो / शकते
- ... करता येत नाही

## Editorial corrections made during the audit

- fixed a repeated-vowel romanization typo in त्याऐवजी
- corrected compound-plural agreement in a possession example
- corrected plural agreement in a quantity example
- normalized recurring learner romanizations such as Majh-, jaeen, kareen, bheteen, sakali, sandhyakali, kay, and avad-
- kept gender/register labels where they materially change a phrase
- kept unrestricted writing as self-check/model-comparison rather than pretending string matching can grade natural Marathi

## Reference basis

The audit cross-checked key structures against Maharashtra government Marathi-learning material, University of Chicago Marathi in Context, Cambridge English–Marathi examples, and Marathi grammar reference material for postpositions, comparison, modals, conditions, and relative/correlative structures.

## Remaining editorial note

Marathi is morphologically rich and colloquial usage varies by speaker, register, and region. The course now has a consistent reference-based learner system, but a future native-speaker editorial review can still improve idiomatic nuance without blocking the functional-intermediate product milestone.
